# ACW ↔ TDI Rulebook

## Division of responsibility

**ACW answers:** Where are we? What is the structural/trend environment?

**TDI answers:** When is momentum/timing quality good enough to care?

## Core interpretation

- **ALIGNED**: ACW and TDI point in the same direction with useful strength.
- **ACW LEADS**: ACW context is stronger; timing is not fully confirmed.
- **TDI LEADS**: TDI timing/momentum moves first; ACW context has not fully confirmed.
- **CONFLICT**: Directional disagreement is preserved; do not average it into fake neutrality.
- **RECOVERY ↑**: Bullish confluence inside still-bearish confirmed LH/LL structure.
- **PULLBACK ↓**: Bearish confluence inside still-bullish confirmed HH/HL structure.

## Default operating mode

Use **Advisory** during calibration. ACW remains independently valid; TDI strengthens or weakens context but does not blindly veto it.

## Safety / decision rule

Indicators provide context and timing evidence. Final trade decisions remain with the trader.
